Free scanners already exist. Why pay?

Scanners flag; they don't fix, verify, or own the outcome. You pay for the confirmed report, the applied fixes, the re-check proof, and someone to blame at 2am before launch. The free scan above shows our standard — the paid work is everything it can't reach.

What access do you need?

Audit: read-only wherever possible (repo read access or a code snapshot, plus a test account). Rescue: a scoped way to apply fixes (PR to your repo, or paired session). Never your production secrets — rotated keys only.

What if my app comes back clean?

You get a proof-of-clean report you can show users and customers — worth the $99 alone — plus the month's re-check free if you take Care.

What does "48h or free" actually mean?
